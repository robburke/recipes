# Resurrecting old tech

Starter prompts for bringing old software, websites and devices back to life. Copy one into your AI assistant and fill in the brackets.

Last updated September 2026.

## Port an old project

From [Legend of the Greasepole: Resurrected](https://robburke.net/side-quests/legend-of-the-greasepole-resurrected/).

```text
I have an old project in [language or platform] that no longer runs. Help me choose a modern target that runs on [the web or phones] without plugins. Before porting anything, build the smallest stub that proves the approach works end to end, and stop for my OK before porting the rest.
```

**A good result:** a tiny working stub on the new platform before any big porting starts, and a clear pause for your decision.

Or play the result: [the Legend of the Greasepole, in your browser](https://robburke.net/gptest/).

## Rebuild an old website

From [LegendWeb: Resurrected](https://robburke.net/side-quests/legendweb-resurrected/).

```text
I have an old website [its URL, or a folder of its files] built with [the old tool]. Propose a plan to rebuild it with a modern static site generator: a simpler structure with fewer pages and at most two levels of navigation, and a list of pages to keep word for word. I'll ask another AI assistant the same question and bring you both plans to combine.
```

**A good result:** two plans that disagree somewhere useful, and a combined plan that takes the better half of each.

## Revive an old device

From [Man, Machine, Drawer](https://robburke.net/side-quests/man-machine-drawer/).

```text
I have an old [device, model and year] that no longer gets updates. Before we change anything, help me find what it can still do: official options, community projects such as alternative apps or firmware, and which of them can be fully undone. Rank them from lowest to highest risk, tell me how to back the device up first, and flag anything that can't be reversed.
```

**A good result:** a ranked list you can work down from the safest option, with a backup step before anything else.

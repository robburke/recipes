# Home and family

Starter prompts for lineups, paperwork and other household jobs. Copy one into your AI assistant and fill in the brackets. None of them needs a login, an account number or a real figure.

Last updated September 2026.

## Make a fair team rotation

From [Full Ted Lasso (is the ROI)](https://robburke.net/side-quests/full-ted-lasso-is-the-roi/).

```text
I coach a kids' [sport] team of [number] players. Make a rotation for the next 3 games that gives every player equal time at every position and a fair spot in the batting order, and gives each player one of [the favourite positions] every game. Use jersey numbers or first initials instead of full names. Put each game in a table I can paste into a shared spreadsheet. Then show how you'd adjust game 1 if [two players] don't show up, without breaking fairness across the three games.
```

**A good result:** three tables you can paste straight into a spreadsheet, and a no-show plan that keeps the fairness intact.

## Get ready for tax time

From [Tax Return Assistant](https://robburke.net/side-quests/tax-return-assistant/).

```text
I do the tax return for a household in [country or province] with [situation: two jobs, kids, childcare, a rental, investments]. Build me a checklist of every slip, receipt and statement I should gather before I see my accountant, grouped by where each one comes from. Then list questions worth asking the accountant this year. Don't ask me for account numbers, logins or any actual figures.
```

**A good result:** a checklist grouped by source that you can work through before the appointment, and a few questions worth asking.

## Sort out which bike you rode

From [SAASpocalypse Now](https://robburke.net/side-quests/saaspocalypse-now/).

```text
I ride [two or three] bikes, and my rides land in [Strava or Garmin Connect] without the right bike attached. Here is a pasted list of my last [20] rides: date, distance, average speed, elevation, and which bike I actually rode. Work out simple rules that tell the bikes apart and show how many of these rides the rules get right. Don't connect to any of my accounts; I'll decide later whether to automate it.
```

**A good result:** a few plain rules and a score for how many past rides they get right, before anything touches your accounts.

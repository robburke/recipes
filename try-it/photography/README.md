# Photography

Starter prompts from three photography side quests. Copy one into your AI assistant and fill in the brackets.

Last updated September 2026.

## Write down your editing taste

From [Brand New Renaissance](https://robburke.net/side-quests/brand-new-renaissance/).

```text
I edit [kind of photos, e.g. kids' sports] in [Lightroom or another editor]. I'll share [5 to 10] before-and-after pairs of my own edits. For each pair, describe what I changed (crop, straightening, white balance, exposure, colour) and guess why. Then write a one-page checklist of my editing taste, split into routine fixes and style choices, and list anything you're unsure of as questions for me.
```

**A good result:** a checklist you recognize as yours, plus a few questions about choices you have never had to explain.

## Brief an edit

From [Vibe Photography](https://robburke.net/side-quests/vibe-photography/).

```text
Here is a photo I took: [attach it]. I took it because [what I remember, what I felt, what I want the edit to bring out]. It's headed for [a 12x16 print, desktop wallpaper or phone background], and I like the look of [a photographer or style]. Propose two or three edit directions, including one adventurous one. For each, list the crop and every adjustment, with the reason each serves what I told you, so I can apply them myself on a copy in [Lightroom, Snapseed or Photos]. If you can operate my editor, work on a virtual copy and leave the masks and sliders for me to inspect.
```

**A good result:** directions that each tie back to why you took the photo, and one you would not have tried on your own.

## Plan a shoot

From [Swim Meet Photographer's Assistant](https://robburke.net/side-quests/swim-meet-photographers-assistant/).

```text
I'm photographing [event: a swim meet, a tournament, a recital] on [date]. Here is the schedule [paste the heat sheet or program], the people I most want to capture, the shots I want to try, and notes on the venue. Build a chronological cheat sheet I can read on my phone: what's happening, where to stand and what to shoot at each point. Include the personal moments I shouldn't miss, not only the technical shots.
```

**A good result:** one page you can scroll on the day, in time order, that you actually check between events.
